## The challenge

Regional buildout was moving, but not always in step with where the network needed to go.
Local decisions and corporate modernization priorities kept drifting apart, costing
consistency and rework.

## The approach

I developed and executed a 4G/5G modernization strategy that mapped regional build plans
directly onto corporate priorities — one roadmap the field and the leadership team could
both point to, with clear sequencing and shared definitions of done.

## The outcome

Regional delivery and network strategy pulled in the same direction, strengthening
operational consistency and making delivery performance more predictable across the region.
