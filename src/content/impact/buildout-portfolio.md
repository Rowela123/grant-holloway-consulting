## The challenge

A carrier needed to accelerate 4G/5G coverage across the Carolinas and Tennessee against
fixed corporate deadlines — a mixed portfolio of macro new-builds, small cells and
in-building systems, with a capital budget that left no room for slippage.

## The approach

I led a team of 13 engineers and took ownership of the full delivery portfolio and its
$15–20M CAPEX. We set a single prioritization model across all three states, ran a weekly
delivery cadence tied to the corporate milestone plan, and made schedule and spend visible
to leadership every week rather than every quarter.

## The outcome

The team delivered against aggressive timelines while holding budget, with a consistent
operating rhythm that removed the guesswork from where each site stood and what came next.
