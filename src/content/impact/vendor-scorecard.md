## The challenge

Vendor responsiveness was inconsistent and escalations dragged. Problems surfaced late,
ownership was fuzzy, and aging tasks quietly pushed delivery dates without anyone deciding
to let them.

## The approach

I built a vendor scorecard that tracked task aging, surfaced performance gaps against clear
thresholds, and gave every open issue an owner and a clock. Reviews moved from anecdote to
evidence, so the conversation shifted from "who is to blame" to "what unblocks this today."

## The outcome

Escalations resolved faster and more predictably, weak spots became visible before they hit
the schedule, and vendor partners had a shared, fair definition of what good looked like.
